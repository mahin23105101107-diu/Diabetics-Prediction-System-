Research Context

About Diabetes Prediction System

Diabetes Prediction System is a research-based project that explores how lifestyle habits and selected health-related information can be used to assess potential diabetes risk through a machine learning approach.

The website provides an interactive questionnaire and visual assessment experience designed to demonstrate the concept of lifestyle-based diabetes prediction.

01

Everyday Lifestyle Signals

Examines age group, weekly exercise consistency, and sweet craving habits using exact survey categories.

02

Non-Invasive Questionnaire

Evaluates family history, high blood pressure background, and 4 observable symptoms without lab tests.

03

Calibrated Random Forest Inference

Runs a 100-tree Random Forest trained on the 994-row study dataset with 5-fold Platt probability calibration directly in the browser.

How the Assessment Flows Dedicated Prediction Workspace
01

Guided Bilingual Questions

9 required questions across 3 steps presented in clear English and Bangla matching the training dataset.

02

Exact One-Hot Encoding & Tree Ensemble

User selections are one-hot encoded into 28 binary indicators and evaluated across all 100 trained Random Forest trees.

03

Prediction & Calibrated Confidence

Displays Low or Higher Predicted Risk alongside a Platt-calibrated confidence percentage, tier, and neutral observations.

Educational research model trained on the project survey dataset (994 deduplicated rows) — not a clinical diagnostic tool.

Notice & Research Scope

Medical Disclaimer & Model Limitations

This website is an educational screening and academic research tool only. Predictions and confidence scores are generated by a Random Forest Classifier trained on a 994-row survey dataset (~51% positive class balance, predominantly respondents aged 18–25) and are not a substitute for professional medical evaluation or laboratory testing (such as fasting glucose or HbA1c). If you have concerns about diabetes or experience symptoms, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Model Specification
